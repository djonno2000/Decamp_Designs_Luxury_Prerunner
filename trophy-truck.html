Chapter 01 — The Goal

Finish when the expensive trucks don't

The Finke Desert Race runs ~226 km each way down the Old Ghan road between Alice Springs and Aputula — a prologue, then two days, same track reversed, cumulative time. First-time big-race trucks finish at well under 5%; about half the field DNFs.

So the north star is simple and unfashionable: finishing is the win. Build for reliability, safety and the driver — a dependable 500–600 hp that lasts, not a grenade 900 hp that strands you.

  • Race — Finke, King's Birthday long weekend (2026 = 50th running)
  • Class — Extreme 2WD, rear-drive only, Motorsport Australia rules
  • Mandatory — MA-spec cage, off-road licence, logbook, fire suit
  • Dual brief — SA road-registered and Finke-capable (GVM ≤ 4,495 kg)

Chapter 03 — The Driveline

LS V8, into a Ford 9"

A used iron 6.0L LS (LQ4/LQ9 dropout, ~$2.5–4.5k) built for reliability over peak power, feeding a Ford 9" centre on a spool with 35-spline billet axles. Cheap, strong, field-serviceable.

Likely naturally aspirated — a cam, intake and a properly tuned stepped / megaphone exhaust get a desert LS to ~480–500 hp with none of a blower's heat and complexity (supercharging stays on the table). Either way, the exhaust is where the real power hides.

The other live decision is the box. TH400 vs ZF 8HP70 — under evaluation, gated on a mate's standalone controller. The 8HP's software-controlled lockup is the prize: lock it on the street, let it slip in the desert to soak driveline hits.

Chapter 04 — Suspension & Roll Control

Geometry first, then the valving

Independent SLA front, solid-axle 4-link rear, on King-style coilovers plus bypass shocks — four of each, fully rebuildable. Tuned for +400 / −260 mm of front wheel travel. A well-tuned mid-tier shock beats a badly-tuned expensive one; that's the cheap edge.

Holding roll without killing compliance is its own engineering problem — the answer here is a steel bar plus exactly one blow-off shock, packaged over the diff.

Read the roll-control deep-dive

  • Front — independent SLA, +400 / −260 mm travel, −9.4° camber gain at full bump
  • Rear — solid axle on a 4-link, 360 / 240 mm, 300 mm articulation
  • Shocks — coilover + bypass ×4 each, rebuildable (King ref / Profender budget)
  • Roll — minimum hydraulic content: steel bar + one blow-off link
Chapter 05 — Wheels & Stance

37s on beadlocks — and a registered set

37×12.50R17 Kenda Klever M/T2 (~$550 each, buy 5–6 with two spares racked low and rearward). Nominal 37"; ~35.5–36" loaded. Beadlock race wheels for the desert, plus a separate road-legal 17" set, because beadlocks aren't road-legal in AU.

Chapter 06 — Build Log

Rolling → Running → Road-legal

Stage 0

Legal & safety foundation

LVES frame classification (the SA rego trap) and the MA-spec cage — before any metal is cut.

Stage 1

Drivetrain core

Resolve TH400 vs 8HP with Decs, then LS + Ford 9" + cooling, built for reliability.

Stage 2

Suspension geometry + tuning

Geometry right first — motion ratio, travel, anti-squat — then valving and rebound.

Stage 3–5

Wheels · systems · nav

37s and the beadlock process, dust-proofing and redundancy, then a simple offline nav setup.

Stage 6–7

Driver + shakedown

Seat time throughout, then a shorter race before Finke — the cure for the <5% first-timer finish rate.
